# Research Principles

1. **Observe, don't claim to read minds.** A facial movement is not an emotion.
2. **Use multiple weak signals.** Prefer gaze + behavior + context + outcome over one facial signal.
3. **Always establish a baseline.**
4. **Measure outcomes, not technical novelty.**
5. **Privacy by design.** Prefer camera → on-device processing → abstract signal → fingerprint.
6. **Human agency.** Adaptation should be predictable, reversible and minimally disruptive.
7. **Avoid over-personalization.** Do not react to every tiny fluctuation.
8. **Research before automation.** Validate useful signals before adding autonomous agents.
9. **Negative findings matter.** Remove signals or interventions that do not add value.
